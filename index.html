Questions, answered.

What is MyLotus?

An iPhone app that writes you one personalized quote, proverb or affirmation every morning, chosen for your goals, your challenges and the tone you respond to.

How does it personalize my phrases?

MyLotus gives you a prompt to paste into Claude or ChatGPT. Your assistant writes a short profile of you from what it already knows, you paste it back, and every phrase is written from that profile. No AI? Answer a one-minute questionnaire instead.

Does it work with ChatGPT and Claude?

Yes, with Claude, ChatGPT or any AI assistant that can answer a prompt. It takes 3 taps: copy, ask your AI, paste.

How much does it cost?

$29.99 per year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 per month. Subscriptions are managed by Apple and can be canceled anytime.

Is my data private?

Your profile is only used to write your phrases. No ads, your data is never sold, and you can delete your account anytime in Settings.
